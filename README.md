# Ali Shavakhi

**M.Sc. Control Systems Engineering, University of Padova.** Writing my master's
thesis at **IT+Robotics** on 6D object pose estimation for industrial bin-picking.
I build ROS 2 systems that see, decide and move — pose estimation, colour

underneath them.

---

## Projects

- **[turtlebot3-navigation-perception](https://github.com/alishavakhi/turtlebot3-navigation-perception)** — ROS 2
  navigation and perception on a simulated TurtleBot 3: AprilTag goal extraction,
  Nav2, a LiDAR corridor-following controller, LiDAR table detection, and a baseline
  versus Kalman-filtered state estimator.
- **[ur5-vision-guided-manipulation](https://github.com/alishavakhi/ur5-vision-guided-manipulation)** —
  vision-guided cube swapping on a simulated UR5: 6-DoF pose recovery from a single
  RGB camera, colour classification, pose/colour fusion with staleness handling, and
  a constraint-aware MoveIt 2 pick-and-place sequence in C++.
- **[traffic-sign-light-detection](https://github.com/alishavakhi/traffic-sign-light-detection)** — one YOLO11
  detector for 33 classes, unified from two datasets whose annotation formats,
  coordinate conventions and label taxonomies have nothing in common.
- **[control-engineering-laboratory](https://github.com/alishavakhi/control-engineering-laboratory)** — five
  applied control laboratories in MATLAB/Simulink: Bode-method PID design with
  experimental identification, state-space tracking with anti-windup, digital control
  by emulation, LQR on a resonant two-mass load, and discrete LQR with integral
  action on a balancing robot.

The simulation environments are vendored, so the robotics repositories build
self-contained. Each one documents what was verified and what was not.
